Welcome! I am a design engineer specializing in new energy power plants, primarily engaged in the design of photovoltaic and energy storage systems. I work in the New Energy Division of [CISDI Electrical Technology Co., Ltd](https://cisdielectric.cn/).

I earned a Master of Engineering degree in Electrical Engineering from the [College of Electrical and Electronic Engineering](https://eee.wzu.edu.cn/) at [Wenzhou University](https://www.wzu.edu.cn/), where I studied under [Professor Guoqiang Zeng](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E6%9B%BE%E5%9B%BD%E5%BC%BA/55625131). My research focused on power systems and their automation, with key areas including optimization theory, smart grids, intrusion detection systems, and neural network models. In addition, I earned a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from the [School of Mechatronics and Vehicle Engineering](http://smve.cqjtu.edu.cn/) at [Chongqing Jiaotong University](https://www.cqjtu.edu.cn/).

I have published a paper on backdoor defense in *IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics & Security* and a conference paper on intrusion detection system design at the 8th IEEE International Conference on Energy Internet (IEEE ICEI 2024).
